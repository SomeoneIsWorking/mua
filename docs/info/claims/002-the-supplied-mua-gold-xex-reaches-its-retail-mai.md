## Claim

The supplied MUA Gold XEX reaches its retail main menu in the existing headless Xenia observation path

## Evidence

A bounded 90-second run issued 1210 1280x720 presents, captured 6 of 6 attempts, accepted four scripted input state changes, initialized the audio client, and showed the full PLAY/XBOX LIVE/OPTIONS/REVIEW menu without a guest crash or device loss.

## What would falsify it

The same exact XEX failing before the menu under the pinned observation binary, any capture becoming uniformly black, or evidence that the captured menu came from a different title/revision.
